# SmCo and cobalt notes (workstream smco_cobalt), as of 2026-11-16

Scope reminder: from 1 Jan 2027 the SmCo test covers "the entire supply chain from mining or production of a cobalt and samarium ore or feedstock, including recycled material, through production of finished magnets". A SmCo magnet made outside China from Chinese samarium or China-refined cobalt fails. Files: 16 smco supplier rows, 35 cobalt supplier rows, 16 excluded, 13 offtakes, 67 sources.

Method caveat: the shared web-search budget ran out after the first SmCo batch. Later rows rest on direct fetches of company pages, ASX/company PDFs, USGS and Apple's smelter list. Several entities could not be checked for 2026 news and carry low confidence (see the last section).

## Who matters at each stage (non-covered-country only)

### SmCo
**Separation (Sm oxide)**
- Lynas Malaysia (Gebeng/Kuantan): first on-spec Sm oxide on 19 Mar 2026, from Mt Weld (Australia) feed. JARE has a firm commitment to buy the equivalent of 50% of all Lynas heavy-rare-earth oxides to 2038 (ASX 19 Mar and 10 Mar 2026).
- Solvay La Rochelle (France): already supplies Sm oxide for Permag via LCM, in "limited volumes" (Nov 2025). Concentrate origin is undisclosed. A Viridis (Brazil) LOI targets new feed by 2028.
- IREL Rare Earths Division Aluva (India, state-owned): lists >99% Sm, Eu and Gd oxides from Indian monazite. Exports are unlikely.
- Planned: Energy Fuels White Mesa (Sm/Eu/Gd circuits "later in 2028", Q3-2026 results) and Ucore Louisiana (US DoW US$4.6m to add Sm/Gd, Sep 2026).

**Metal (Sm metal)**
- Less Common Metals (Cheshire, UK; owned by USA Rare Earth since 18 Nov 2025): the only established Western Sm metal maker. In Q3 2026 samarium metal shortages limited output. Furnaces adding about 30% capacity are due in Q3 2027 (USAR Q3-2026 release).
- LSCV / LS Eco Energy (Ho Chi Minh City): 240 t/yr Sm metal, with trial production in Dec 2026 on Lynas oxide (Korea Times, 10 Nov 2026; Lynas framework agreement, 26 Mar 2026).
- Planned: REalloys/Terves (Euclid, Ohio), a 300 t/yr Sm+Gd metal plant at the design stage under a DLA contract of up to US$1.7m (Mar 2026).

**Alloy (SmCo alloy/powder)**
- LCM (Cheshire): SmCo alloys. The company calls itself "the sole Western provider" of SmCo metal for defence.
- Santoku (Kobe area, Japan; part of Proterial Magnetics in 2026): SmCo alloys (single trade-press source).
- Magnet makers that alloy in-house: Arnold Lupfig and EEC (unconfirmed for EEC).

**Magnet**
- Electron Energy Corp / Permag (Landisville PA): calls itself the only US SmCo producer. It is AS9100D certified and ITAR-registered, and is more than doubling capacity (Sep 2025).
- Arnold Magnetic Technologies: Lupfig, Switzerland (RECOMA; capacity-constrained); Rochester NY (finishing); a new SmCo line in Amata City, Thailand, due mid-2027.
- Vacuumschmelze, Hanau (VACOMAX): Energy Fuels' US$1.9bn acquisition is pending, with closing expected early 2027.
- Magneti Ljubljana, Slovenia (Remag SmCo5/Sm2Co17): owner and inputs are undisclosed.

### Cobalt
**Refined metal (for SmCo alloying and superalloys)**
- Vale Long Harbour (NL) and Port Colborne (ON): electrolytic cobalt rounds. Vale lists superalloys and magnetic materials among the uses.
- Glencore Nikkelverk (Kristiansand) and Murrin Murrin (WA): cobalt cathode and metal. Norway supplied 26% of US imports of cobalt metal, oxide and salts in 2021-24 (USGS).
- Sumitomo Metal Mining Niihama (Japan): electrolytic cobalt "used for magnetic materials and carbide tools", fed by SMM's own Philippine HPAL plants.
- Managem (Morocco): CMBA cobalt cathode from the Bou-Azzer primary mine, 650 t in 2025.
- Umicore Olen (Belgium): cobalt metals, chemicals and powders. It is one of only two non-China cobalt refiners on Apple's Apr 2025 list.
- Coming: Jervois São Miguel Paulista (Brazil) restart, 2,000 t/yr cathode from late 2027.

**Refined chemicals**
- Electra (Ontario): 5,120 t/yr contained Co as sulfate, with commercial production in H2 2027.
- Terrafame (Finland): cobalt sulphate.
- Jervois Finland and Umicore Finland (Kokkola).

**Powder**
- Jervois Finland (Kokkola): R-Series 0.9-1.5 um and S-Series 0.8-4.5 um cobalt powders. US-owned (Millstreet Capital) since May 2025.
- Umicore Tool Materials: cobalt and pre-alloyed powders; the plant site is not stated.

**Mining and intermediates (non-Chinese-controlled)**
- Glencore KCC and Mutanda (DRC).
- SMM Coral Bay and Taganito (Philippines; captive).
- Managem Bou-Azzer (captive).
- Eagle Mine (Michigan): concentrate is exported.

**Recycling**
- Redwood Materials (Nevada; battery materials).
- Jervois Finland and Umicore: hardmetal scrap and sludge to cobalt powder.
- Planned: Nth Cycle (DOE selection) and Li-Cycle Rochester (stalled).

## The real bottleneck
- **Samarium metal is the tightest stage.** One Western producer is operating (LCM), and its parent reported samarium metal shortages limiting output in Q3 2026. Its next capacity step (+30%) is Q3 2027.
  - The only other near-term addition is LSCV: 240 t/yr from 2027, likely tolled for Lynas.
  - REalloys (300 t/yr Sm+Gd) is still at design.
- **Samarium oxide is the second constraint.** Lynas only began Sm oxide in Mar 2026 and has committed half its heavy-rare-earth output to JARE. Solvay's volumes are "limited" and its feed origin is undisclosed. No US Sm separation operates before 2028 (Energy Fuels; Ucore after its first machine in H1 2027).
- **Cobalt metal is not physically scarce outside China, but compliance documentation is.**
  - The DRC mined about 73% of world cobalt in 2025 and Indonesia 14% (USGS MCS 2026). China remains the leading refined producer.
  - Non-China refiners buying DRC or Indonesian intermediates must prove their feed did not come from Chinese-owned mines (e.g., CMOC's TFM/KFM) or Chinese-partnered HPAL plants.
  - Apple's Apr 2025 list shows only two non-China cobalt refiners (Umicore Olen, Umicore Finland) against 13 in China.
- **The defense-grade cobalt market is thin.** DLA's Aug 2025 tender sought alloy-grade cobalt from three specific Western brands certified for rotating engine parts: 7,480 t over 5 years, up to US$500m. It was cancelled on 24 Oct 2025 (Fastmarkets; USGS).

## Uncommitted non-China capacity for 2027
- **Sm metal: unknown.** The only quantified new capacity is LSCV's 240 t/yr. It is framed as a Lynas processing arrangement, so free volume is unclear. LCM is capacity-short.
- **Sm oxide: unknown.** Lynas does not publish Sm capacity, and 50% of its heavy-rare-earth output is firm to JARE. Solvay gives no figures.
- **SmCo magnets: unknown.** No producer publishes capacity. EEC is doubling, and Arnold Thailand doubles Arnold's magnet capacity by mid-2027.
- **Cobalt metal:**
  - Jervois SMP adds about 2,000 t/yr of uncommitted cobalt cathode from late 2027, subject to restart risk and feed screening.
  - Electra's 5,120 t/yr is sulfate (about 60% to LGES through 2029, and WMC is proposed as exclusive marketer), so it is not SmCo-grade.
  - Existing refiners (Vale, Glencore, SMM, Managem, Umicore) sell mostly under direct contracts. Free volume is unknown.

## Top 10 broker prospects
1. Electron Energy Corp (Permag) - Landisville PA - the only US SmCo maker, expanding, PE-owned and seeking DFARS-compliant inputs.
2. LSCV / LS Eco Energy - Ho Chi Minh City - 240 t/yr Sm metal from 2027 on Lynas oxide, aimed at defense SmCo demand.
3. Jervois SMP - São Paulo - 2,000 t/yr uncommitted cobalt cathode from late 2027, with a US-controlled owner.
4. Jervois Finland - Kokkola - fine cobalt powders and chemicals for aerospace/defense. US-owned and pursuing US programmes; feed origin must be documented.
5. Managem - Morocco - small, clean primary-cobalt cathode (650 t) for superalloy users.
6. Arnold Magnetic Technologies - Lupfig / Amata City (Thailand) - SmCo capacity doubling by mid-2027. It must replace its Chinese Sm (Ganzhou JV) with LCM/Solvay material.
7. Less Common Metals - Cheshire - Sm metal and SmCo alloy. Supply is tight; watch the Q3 2027 expansion and the H1 2027 repricing of legacy contracts.
8. Solvay - La Rochelle - Sm oxide that it can scale. A broker could add value by verifying feed origin.
9. Vale Base Metals - Long Harbour / Port Colborne - clean Canadian electrolytic cobalt rounds for SmCo melters.
10. Magneti Ljubljana - Slovenia - independent EU SmCo maker. Ownership and inputs need diligence first.

## Gaps: stages with zero or near-zero compliant supply
- **SmCo recycling:** no verified non-China SmCo swarf or scrap recycler in the file. This matters because the FY2026 NDAA recycled-SmCo exemption needs US milling and sintering.
- **Sm metal:** one operating Western plant (LCM), whose oxide sourcing is not fully disclosed, plus one commissioning (LSCV).
- **Sm separation in the US:** none operating until late 2028.
- **US refined cobalt:** none operating. USGS says US cobalt supply is imports plus scrap, and Eagle's concentrate is exported. Electra (Canada) starts H2 2027; Jervois' US refinery has no site or date.
- **Fully documented SmCo magnet chains:** no SmCo magnet row is rated `likely_compliant`. Every magnet maker either has disclosed Chinese Sm (Arnold) or has undisclosed Sm or cobalt origins.
