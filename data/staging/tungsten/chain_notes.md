## The real bottleneck (which stage, why, numbers with sources)

The bottleneck is **non-China chemical conversion (APT and oxide) and the primary concentrate behind it.** In the US it is the lack of an independent APT plant.

**Supply concentration.**
- World mine output in 2025 was about 85,000 t W. China produced 67,000 t, Russia about 2,000 t and North Korea about 2,000 t, so covered countries supplied about 84% (USGS MCS 2026).
- Non-covered mine supply was about 14,000 t W. That includes Kazakhstan (2,400 t), whose new Boguty output is reported as Chinese-owned. Excluding it leaves about 11,600 t W of non-covered, non-Chinese-controlled mine supply.

**US conversion capacity.**
- US conversion of concentrate to APT/oxide is limited to GTP Towanda (Austrian-owned, mostly captive), Niagara Refining (captive to Sumitomo Electric) and Kennametal (captive).
- The first independent US APT plant (Springer, about 4,000 t APT/yr) is not due before 2H 2028 to 1H 2029.
- Elmet buys concentrate (Masan, EQR) and leans on Masan for processing.

**Prices** (signals of the squeeze after China's Feb 2025 export controls):
- Rotterdam APT rose from US$331 to US$675/mtu during 2025 (USGS).
- European APT averaged about US$2,800/MTU in Q3 2026 (Almonty Q3 2026).
- Fastmarkets APT mid-price was US$304,000/t WO3 on 12 Jun 2026 (Guardian PFS).

**Stockpile.** The DLA FY2025 Annual Materials Plan listed potential disposals of 499 t (W content) of tungsten ores and concentrates and potential acquisitions of 2,041 t tungsten. The FY2026 plan was not released (USGS MCS 2026). A DLA IDIQ of up to US$2bn with Elmet (Sep 2026) will absorb new concentrate and sodium tungstate once supply exists.

## Gaps: stages with zero or near-zero compliant supply

- **Independent US APT/oxide:** none operating until Springer (2H 2028 to 1H 2029). Existing US chemical capacity is captive (GTP, Niagara, Kennametal).
- **Non-China primary concentrate:** small and concentrated in about 8 mines. Most new mines start 2027-2029 (Hemerdon 2027; Springer Q4 2027; El Moto Q1 2029; Mactung and Sisson later).
- **Korea:** a mine (Sangdong) exists but no operating oxide/APT plant yet (Yeongwol is planned). GB Innovation's concentrate is toll-converted in Vietnam.
- **Recycled exception:** recyclers in Taiwan (Jing Yuan), Brazil (Cronimet), India (WBH Chiplun) and Ireland (Tungco Limerick) are outside the US and qualifying countries. Scrap-origin documentation is generally not public.
- **Feed verification:** several RMI-conformant Asian smelters (Lianyou x2, Philippine Bonway/Chuangxin, Tungsten Vietnam JSC, KENEE) have unverified ownership and feed. Tungamoy Metals (Korea) appears to be a Xiamen Tungsten affiliate.
