# Molybdenum notes (workstream molybdenum), as of 2026-11-16

**Rule status.** Molybdenum is a covered material **by statute** (10 U.S.C. 4872(f), added by FY2026 NDAA, Pub. L. 119-60 sec. 844(a), 18 Dec 2025), on the same timeline as the other materials (mined/refined/separated test for contracts awarded from 1 Jan 2027 under DFARS 252.225-7961, deliveries from 1 Jan 2027 under 252.225-7052). **It is not yet in the DFARS clause text**, and DFARS has not defined which molybdenum forms are covered. Forms most likely to be treated as covered: molybdenum **metal** - powders, unwrought metal, mill products (sheet, plate, rod, bar, wire, foil, billets) and alloys (TZM, Mo-Re, Mo-La/ML, MHC, Mo-W) and parts containing them; the pending House FY2027 NDAA (H.R. 8800 sec. 1803) names "molybdenum metals, powders, and alloys" as Tier 1 from 18 Dec 2027, with a 50%-of-cost "domestically owned entity" test. Whether **FeMo, technical oxide and other alloying additions** (consumed in steel and superalloys) are covered is unresolved; under the general 2027 test every upstream stage (mine, roaster) of a covered form counts, so concentrate origin and roaster location must be traced for metal products too. Statutory recycled-Mo exemption: recycled feed produced outside covered nations, with melting/processing in the US or a qualifying country (DFARS text not yet aligned). See `docs/scope.md`.

**Method limit.** The shared web-search budget ran out early in this workstream; most rows after Molymet were verified only by direct fetches of company sites, filings and USGS. 2026 news could not be searched for those entities (stated in row notes).

## The real bottleneck
**Molybdenum metal powder and mill products, and proof of the oxide feeding them.** Upstream is not tight outside China: ~61% of 2025 mine output (USGS) and most non-China roasting (Molymet ~94,000 t Mo/yr capacity; FCX converts most of its own 92 Mlb (~41,700 t) 2025 output in its own plants; Langeloth at roughly one-third utilisation) are outside covered countries. By contrast, only a handful of non-China plants make Mo powder and semis, only one publishes a powder capacity (Molymet Germany 1,700 t/yr), and most (Elmet, A.L.M.T., Amaero, Toho Kinzoku, AMG) do not disclose where their oxide/powder feed comes from. China imposed export controls on Mo powders in Feb 2025 (USGS MCS 2026), and the US has no publicly quantified conventional Mo-powder capacity; Elmet calls itself the only US-owned integrated W/Mo producer, and DoW committed US$450M to it in Sep 2026. Prices: FCX realised US$32.46/lb in Q3 2026 vs US$24.07/lb a year earlier.

## Uncommitted non-China capacity for 2027 (best estimate)
- **Roasting:** about **27-29 Mlb/yr (~12,000-13,000 t Mo) of idle roasting capacity at Langeloth** (40 Mlb nameplate minus 11-13 Mlb 2026 roasting guidance; Centerra). Thompson Creek (~13 Mlb/yr from mid-2027) will fill part of it, leaving roughly 14-16 Mlb/yr for third-party feed by 2028. Basis: company figures; actual availability depends on feed purchases.
- **Concentrate:** largely committed (captive or long-term tolling). Gibraltar (mine-gate sales, ~2-3 Mlb/yr) and Erdenet (>5,000 t concentrate/yr, routing undisclosed) are the main non-captive streams found.
- **Metal powder and mill products:** **unknown** - no plant discloses spare capacity.
