## Who matters at each stage (3-8 bullets per stage, non-covered-country only)

**mining_concentrate**
- Global Advanced Metals (GAM): holds the tin-tantalum rights at Greenbushes (concentrate produced for GAM by Talison from four plants) and runs its own tantalum recovery plants at Wodgina; upgrades both at its Greenbushes secondary plant. All captive to Boyertown. Talison is controlled through Tianqi-led TLEA (IGO ASX, 2026-02-12).
- PLS Pilgangoora (Australia): 220,585 lb tantalite concentrate produced in FY26, 46,233 lb in Sep Q 2026; no current tantalite offtake disclosed (the 2017 GAM deal expired).
- AMG Brasil, Mibra mine (Nazareno, MG): tantalum is a growing profit line (Q3 2026: Ta prices about 2.5x a year earlier); TANIOBIS holds preferential purchase rights after JX's investment.
- Andrada Mining, Uis (Namibia): 37 t tantalum concentrate in FY2026 (51 t FY2025); markets "conflict-free, fully traceable" tantalum.
- Trinity Metals, Musha (Rwanda): industrial tin-tantalum mine; US DFC technical-assistance grant US$3.865M (2024); TechMet equity.
- Eastinco / Aterian (Rwanda): first exports Feb 2026 under an exclusive Wogen trading partnership.
- Flag, not a target: Mineracao Taboca (Pitinga, Brazil) has been owned by China's CNMC since 2025; Tanco (Canada) is owned by Sinomine and reports no tantalum output (USGS shows none for Canada).

**chemical_processing**
- GAM Boyertown, PA: ore-to-metal integrated, so its K-salt/oxide stage is captive.
- TANIOBIS (JX Advanced Metals): Goslar and Map Ta Phut (K2TaF7, Ta2O5), Laufenburg (chlorides). Intermediates are bought only from RMAP-conformant smelters (origins not named); DRC/Rwanda sourcing suspended since end-2023.
- AMG Brasil, Sao Joao del Rei: tantalum and niobium oxides fed from Mibra.
- MPIL, Taloja (India): small family-owned K-salt/oxide producer, RMAP conformant.
- NPM Silmet, Sillamae (Estonia; Neo Performance Materials): Ta2O5 and TaCl5 from columbite and secondary feed (origin not disclosed).
- Mitsui Kinzoku (Omuta) and Taki Chemical (Hyogo): Japanese oxide/carbide refiners, feed undisclosed.
- KEMET de Mexico (Matamoros; YAGEO): K-salt, captive.
- Excluded on compliance grounds although located in Kazakhstan: Ulba. Its 2025-26 due-diligence report records supplier audits at Solikamsk Magnesium Works and Lovozero (Russia) in April 2026 and no primary tantalum purchases in the period.

**metal_powder_ingot**

**alloy_mill_products**

## The real bottleneck (which stage, why, numbers with sources)
- **The bottleneck is primary metal/powder (and the K-salt step behind it) with a documentable non-covered feed.** US tantalum metal and powder imports in 2021-24 came 47% from China and 16% from Kazakhstan (USGS MCS 2026). Kazakhstan's supplier is Ulba, which now uses Russian loparite-derived feed (Ulba DD report 2025-26). So about 63% of the 2021-24 import base comes from sources that would fail the 2027 test on current information. The rest came 25% from Germany and 4% from Thailand (TANIOBIS) plus other sources.
- **Only one US ore-to-ingot processor has a transparent Australian feed: GAM Boyertown.** DLA's sole-source justification says the same. TANIOBIS is the only other large non-China integrated group, and its feed of intermediates and slags is not traceable by origin in public documents.
- **Mine supply that can be documented is thin.** In 2025 world mine output was about 2,500 t Ta. DRC (1,300 t), Rwanda (400 t) and Nigeria (390 t) supplied about 84%, mostly artisanal and mostly processed in China. Australia (50 t), Brazil (190 t, of which the Taboca share is now Chinese-owned) and Namibia/Rwanda industrial mines are the documentable non-covered sources (USGS MCS 2026; company filings).
- **Prices show the squeeze.** Tantalite averaged $180/kg Ta2O5 in 2025 (USGS). Reuters reported 20-year highs in March 2026 after Rubaya landslides (via Andrada FY2026 report), and AMG put prices at about 2.5x a year earlier in Q3 2026.

## Uncommitted non-China capacity for 2027 (best estimate with stated basis; say "unknown" if you cannot support a number)
- **Unknown.** No non-China processor publishes tantalum capacity, and offtake books are private.
- The only quantified flow that looks uncommitted is PLS tantalite: about 220,585 lb of concentrate a year (FY26), with grade and Ta2O5 content not disclosed and no buyer disclosed.
- New processing capacity is relative only:
  - TANIOBIS Map Ta Phut reaches about 1.5x its March-2022 capacity from 2027-H1.
  - GAM plans to expand its purification lines by 2027 (trade press). GAM's EXIM $25M (Aug 2026) is for Ta/Nb production at Boyertown.
  - Globe Metals' Kanyika (Malawi) targets 65 t/yr Ta2O5 from 2028 at the earliest, so it is outside the 2027 window.

## Gaps: stages with zero or near-zero compliant supply
- **US metal/powder.** GAM Boyertown is the only US source with a documented non-covered ore chain. Materion Newton is US-located but its feed is undisclosed.
- **Merchant K-salt/oxide with a transparent feed.** Almost everything is captive (GAM, KEMET, TANIOBIS) or opaque on feed (Mitsui, Taki, NPM Silmet, MPIL). AMG Brasil is the clearest merchant case.
- **Mill products and targets.** Capacity exists (ATI, Materion, Plansee, STEREON, Solstice, JX), but each buys Ta feed of undisclosed origin. Compliance therefore depends on the upstream smelter, which brokers would need to trace.
- **Recycling.** It is the statutory exemption route, but no recycler publishes scrap origin or volumes.
- **Mine side.** Documentable non-covered, non-conflict concentrate is a few hundred tonnes of Ta a year, much of it captive to GAM.
