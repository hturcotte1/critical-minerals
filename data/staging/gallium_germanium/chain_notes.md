# Gallium and germanium notes (workstream gallium_germanium), as of 2026-11-16

Scope reminder: gallium and germanium become covered materials under 10 U.S.C. 4872 on 18 Dec 2027 (FY2026 NDAA sec. 844(b)). There is no DFARS text yet. Recycled Ga/Ge gets the statutory recycled exemption from that date if the recycled feed is produced outside a covered nation and all processing is in the US or a DFARS 225.003 qualifying country. Belgium, Germany, Greece, Japan, Canada, Australia and the UK qualify. Slovakia, South Korea, Kazakhstan and the DRC are not on the list (https://www.acquisition.gov/dfars/225.003-definitions.).

Method note: the shared WebSearch budget ran out partway through. Later facts come from direct fetches of primary pages: USGS MCS 2026 and Minerals Yearbook 2022, the AXT 10-K, company sites and DFARS. Anything that needed a fresh 2026 news search is listed under "What could not be verified".

## China export-control status (chain-specific check)
- **3 Jul 2023:** China announced export licensing for gallium, germanium and their compounds, effective 1 Aug 2023 (AXT 10-K FY2025, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1051627/000143774926008612/axti20251231_10k.htm; USGS MCS 2026).
- **3 Dec 2024:** China banned exports of gallium, germanium, antimony and superhard materials to the US, effective the same day (AXT 10-K; USGS MCS 2026).
- **Nov 2025:** China suspended the US-specific ban for one year, "through Nov. 27, 2026". The licensing regime and the bar on US military end users stayed in place (USGS MCS 2026 gallium; Metal Tech News, 24-25 Aug 2026).
- **Effect on germanium:** China's germanium metal exports for Jan-Sep 2025 were 7,520 kg, against 18,787 kg in 2024 and 36,656 kg in 2023. The 2025 exports went to Russia (28%), Belgium (26%), Germany (26%) and Japan (18%) (USGS MCS 2026 germanium). A Tradium summary of customs data put April 2026 exports at effectively zero (Metal Tech News, Aug 2026).
- **Prices:**
  - Germanium metal in Europe averaged $4,100/kg in 2025, up from $1,991/kg (USGS).
  - It was about $6,300/kg in late July 2026 (Metal Tech News).
  - Fastmarkets Rotterdam spot was about $10,500/kg for germanium and $2,650/kg for gallium on 26 Jun 2026 (Blue Moon 6-K, Nov 2026).
- **Open item:** the suspension lapses on 27 Nov 2026, 11 days after this note. I could not verify whether it has been extended.

## Who matters at each stage (non-covered-country only)
### Gallium: primary_extraction
- **Alcoa (Wagerup, WA):**
  - 100 t/yr design capacity.
  - FID 14 Jul 2026; ground broken 24 Aug 2026.
  - DoW IBAS equity of about $174M, plus Export Finance Australia and Japan (JAGA, the Sojitz-JOGMEC JV).
  - Offtake goes to partners pro rata, so it is effectively committed.
- **METLEN (Aluminium of Greece):**
  - 50 t/yr; commercial production from 3Q 2027, full rate in 2028.
  - EU CRMA strategic project; EIB loan of EUR90M.
  - About 41% sold so far (a US tech company ~25%, a Japanese chemical company up to 16%).
- **ATALCO (Gramercy, LA):**
  - The only US alumina refinery.
  - Pilot circuit in testing; first US primary gallium expected fall 2026; up to 50 t/yr after expansion.
  - DoW preferred equity of $150M, raised to a reported ~$400M.
  - DLA stockpile IDIQ of up to $350M for up to 55.6 t of gallium over a five-year ordering period. This is a ceiling, not a guaranteed purchase (Argus, 11 Nov 2026).
- **ERG (Pavlodar, Kazakhstan):** 15 t/yr, start targeted 3Q 2026, sold long-term to Mitsubishi RtM Japan. ERG's ties were not verified.
- **Rio Tinto (Vaudreuil/Jonquiere, QC):**
  - Pilot plant 2027, then a demo plant of up to 4 t/yr; commercial 40 t/yr is only a possibility.
  - Funded by NRCan (C$18.95M) and Quebec (C$7M).
- **DOWA / Akita Zinc (Iijima, Japan):** the only operating non-covered primary source, captive. Japan produced about 3 t in 2025 (USGS).
- **Later or R&D projects:** Korea Zinc Crucible (Clarksville, 2029+), ElementUSA (bauxite residue, DPA demo), and three DOE TRACE-Ga prototypes (PHNX, Found Energy, Kunin).

## The real bottleneck
- **Gallium: primary extraction.** This is the binding constraint.
  - In 2025 China produced about 99% of the world's ~900 t of primary low-purity gallium.
  - Outside China, only Japan (~3 t) and Russia (~6 t, a covered country) produced any (USGS MCS 2026).
  - Compliant primary gallium therefore came to about 3 t in 2025. US reported consumption was 19 t (USGS).
  - Every non-China refiner (DOWA, Indium, CMK) and wafer maker depends on crude gallium that is mostly Chinese, unless it buys from the new projects.
  - The new projects add about 219 t/yr of announced non-China capacity (Alcoa 100, METLEN 50, ATALCO 50, ERG 15, Rio Tinto demo 4). None of it was at full rate in 2026, and much is already committed.
- **Germanium: primary supply and traceability.**
  - Production data are not published (USGS).
  - Non-China primary output rests mainly on Teck (undisclosed) and Umicore (undisclosed; STL "up to 30 t/yr" potential), with Korea Zinc adding about 10 t/yr from 2027.
  - Russia (JSC Germanium about 20 t/yr; Germanium & Applications up to 21 t/yr) and China (Yunnan Chihong alone produced 55.9 t in 2022) dominate.
  - Even Western refiners may be using Chinese germanium: Belgium and Germany took 52% of China's 2025 germanium metal exports (USGS). So lot-level origin is the practical bottleneck for DFARS compliance.

## Uncommitted non-China capacity for 2027 (best estimate with stated basis)
- **Gallium:**
  - Calendar 2027: probably under ~15 t of uncommitted, non-China primary gallium. This is not a firm number.
  - Basis:
    - METLEN starts in 3Q 2027 with about 59% not yet publicly contracted, but says it is "almost certain" to sell out before start-up.
    - ATALCO's pilot output is undisclosed. DoW holds equity in it, and DLA has a stockpile IDIQ for up to 55.6 t over five years, so early output is likely to go to DLA first.
    - ERG and Alcoa output is committed.
  - From 2028: up to about 29 t/yr if METLEN's unsold share stays open, plus whatever ATALCO's expansion yields. Treat as unknown.
- **Germanium:**
  - Korea Zinc's about 10 t/yr Ge equivalent (crude Ge from 1Q 2027; 5N GeO2 from 2H 2027) is the only identified block of new output that is uncommitted beyond a non-binding Lockheed MoU.
  - Teck's doubling has no date. STL goes to Umicore. 5N Plus is mostly captive.
  - Basis: company and trade-press statements in the rows. Spot availability from Teck and Umicore is unknown.

## Top 10 broker prospects
1. METLEN - Aluminium of Greece gallium plant: 50 t/yr from 3Q 2027, clean EU chain, about 59% not yet publicly contracted.
2. ATALCO - Gramercy, LA: first US primary gallium (pilot fall 2026, 50 t/yr target). The only commitment found is the DLA stockpile IDIQ (up to 55.6 t over five years), so most of the target volume is still open. Check DoW rights.
3. Korea Zinc / KEMCO - Onsan germanium: new uncommitted Ge and 5N GeO2 in 2027, only a non-binding MoU. Confirm zinc-concentrate origin.
4. Lattice Materials - Bozeman, MT: small ITAR-registered Ge crystal grower expanding with DPA money. A good fit for defense optics buyers.
5. Indium Corporation - Rome/Utica, NY: the only US high-purity gallium refiner, building non-China feed (Rio Tinto, Ames, Metallium). Could toll-refine compliant crude.
6. CMK s.r.o. - Zarnovica, Slovakia: small 7N gallium refiner and GaAs maker that could refine non-China crude (e.g., METLEN). Not a qualifying country for the recycled exemption.
7. 5N Plus - St. George, UT: US Ge wafers and optics with three DoD/DoW awards. Supply is backlog-constrained and feed traceability is needed.
8. Teck - Trail, BC: the cleanest primary GeO2/GeCl4 source. Large, and expansion is undated.
9. Blue Moon Metals - Apex, UT: Ge/Ga ore (direct shipping from Q3 2027), seeking buyers. Teck holds marketing rights.
10. ElementUSA - Gramercy, LA: DPA-funded Ga/Sc demonstration from bauxite residue, uncommitted. No dates.

## Gaps: stages with zero or near-zero compliant supply
- **Gallium primary_extraction:** near zero until Alcoa, METLEN and ATALCO ramp up (2026-2028). The only operating non-covered source is about 3 t/yr in Japan, and it is captive.
- **Gallium refining_high_purity:** refiners exist outside China, but their crude feed is mostly Chinese. Compliant refined gallium depends on pairing the new primary projects with these refiners. No such toll arrangements were found.
- **Gallium compounds (TMGa, GaCl3):** non-China makers exist (Nouryon, Dow, Neo, SAFC), but none has a traced gallium origin. Data are stale.
- **Germanium:** each stage has non-China suppliers, but all of them use feed of undisclosed origin except Teck (Red Dog) and STL (DRC).
